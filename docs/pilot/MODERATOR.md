# Moderated task script

Start: explain the beta, actual build/modes, consent and stopping. Do not enable
AI/search for the participant. Use a tagged fictional test account and confirm
that the designated deletion data are disposable. Do not place a crisis call.

Ask: “Think of the last time you reflected on a day and tried to choose a next
step. What happened? What did you use? Where did you get stuck? What would you
not want to share?” Record only minimal neutral notes with permission.

Task 1 (5 minutes): “Write the supplied fictional entry, save it, leave this page,
refresh, and find exactly what you saved.” Observe authoritative receipt/retrieval.
A button press is not proof. Stop the first-attempt clock at the cap before helping.

Task 2 (7 minutes): “Explain the reflection choices. Use the mode you choose on
this fictional entry. Choose an activity or decline. If you choose one, refresh
and find the saved activity and where you would report what you tried.” Record
requested/actual mode. A suggestion is not confirmation; expiry is not performance.
Record a valid decline neutrally and do not count it as completed action selection.

Task 3 (6 minutes): “Export the fictional saved data and open the result. Tell me
what it includes. Explain how erasing saved data differs from closing an account.
Erase only the designated disposable data and verify they are gone. Can you still
sign in?” If hosted closure is disabled, disclose that gate; do not simulate a pass.

For every group record: unassisted success / assisted success / failure / stopped,
monotonic elapsed seconds, cap reached, assistance, observed error, selected build
and mode. Never overwrite the first attempt with a retest. Ask the participant to
explain consequences in their own words without coaching first.

Close: ask one optional concrete usefulness question: “Was a particular task useful
to you, and what made it useful or unhelpful?” Keep any answer in the restricted
feedback sheet. Ask separately whether they want to enroll in a fresh seven-day
pilot. Record non-enrollment without implying consent or failure.
