# Participant information — draft for the moderator

JournalPulse is a research beta for private writing, optional reflection and an
optional activity. We want to learn whether the workflow is understandable and
useful. It is not therapy or a crisis service. You must be an adult and may stop,
decline an activity or withdraw without explanation. Use fictional or non-sensitive
writing. Do not test account closure on your ordinary account.

Saving does not require AI. Simple uses scripted behavior. AI reflection requires
a separate choice about the specific text shared with the configured provider.
Search requires a separate approval for a general topic. Declining any of these
choices does not prevent journaling. Revocation prevents later dispatch; it cannot
recall a request already sent. The moderator will identify the actual enabled
build, provider boundaries and any disabled capabilities before you begin.

With your permission, the moderator records task outcomes, assistance, duration,
chosen/actual mode and minimal neutral observations under a pseudonym. We do not
record journal text, action titles, mood, credentials or private screenshots.
Recording audio/video is optional and requires a separate explicit agreement;
the default is no recording. Optional usefulness feedback is separately restricted.
Participation, pilot enrollment, optional feedback and recording are distinct choices.

A seven-day pilot is a new voluntary enrollment after the session, not automatic.
No daily use is required. Each participant has a 168-hour observation window.
Missing observations remain unknown and are not a judgment about the participant.

Before consent the moderator supplies their actual contact, build, cohort dates,
research-note owner and deletion date. You may ask them to withdraw research data:
collection stops and linked notes/mapping are deleted within 24 hours. Raw research
records/mapping are deleted no later than 30 days after cohort close. Anonymous
summaries are recomputed. Product saved-data erasure and account closure are
separate app operations; this research procedure does not promise deletion of
provider copies or override product/backup retention.

Record each actual yes/no choice privately with consent version
`pilot-consent-2026-10-24.1` and time: moderated session; optional feedback;
separate recording; later pilot enrollment. A blank form means NOT_OBTAINED.
